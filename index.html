The 7 Arkana Classes

Cyber Blade
Melee / Off-Tank & DPS (Weapon: Lasersword)
Combats enemies with an energetic lasersword. He combines the exceptional agility of an assassin with the steadfast durability of a front-line vanguard. Cyberblades are Arkana specifically engineered to resemble well-balanced human males – standing as the culmination of years of development, they exhibit superior physical and mental capabilities, granting an ideal balance in offense and defense.
Sentinel
Ranged / Agile DPS (Weapon: 2 Pistols)
A true master of ranged weaponry. Lightning-fast and deceptively harmless by design, most enemies don't even realize what hit them until it is far too late. She skillfully evades hostile attacks using acrobatic leaps while unleashing non-stop volleys of bullets upon the battlefield.
Medic
Healer / Support (Weapon: Aegis Cannon)
The indispensable backbone of support on the battlefield. Equipped with the heavy Aegis Cannon, the Medic class can deploy devastating defensive barriers, restore the health of injured allies, and project targeted energy bursts to keep enemies at a distance when necessary.
Defender
Melee / Main Tank (Weapon: Power Shield & Sword)
The ultimate fortress on the front lines. Defender Arkana possess extremely high defensive values and excel at drawing enemy hostility. Utilizing a massive power shield, they absorb tremendous amounts of incoming damage to ensure the survival of the entire squad.
Shadow Walker
Assassin & Burst DPS (Weapon: Claws)
Masters of concealment and sudden ambushes. They possess the unique ability to cloak themselves mid-combat, stalking their prey silently. With their razor-sharp claw weapons, they inflict massive burst damage from the shadows, making them perfect for eliminating key hostile targets like enemy healers within seconds.
Whipper
Mid-Range / AoE DPS & CC (Weapon: Whip)
Dominates the battlefield from a tactical mid-range distance. Utilizing her advanced technological whip, she inflicts heavy area-of-effect (AoE) damage to large groups of opponents simultaneously, making her an absolute champion for PvE grinding. She features powerful crowd control (CC) mechanics to pull, stun, or silence hostiles.
Punisher
Ranged / Heavy DPS (Weapon: Rifle)
Unmatched, brutal firepower from maximum range. The Punisher deploys heavy machine guns (BFG) and portable rocket launchers to carpet-bomb entire areas and obliterate strategic points. Although her heavy attacks require slightly longer execution times, her raw destructive capabilities represent the absolute pinnacle of DPS.

The Factions

Royal Guards

Royal Guard Arkana share the same sense of honor, duty, and loyalty that is associated with their Commanders. They embrace their purpose as living weapons, honing their talents to become the ultimate protectors of humanity's return.

Free Knights

Free Knight Arkana embody the rebellious, independent spirit of their faction, always attempting to outdo each other in strength and skill. Their Commanders strongly encourage this competitive streak, with some even promising the Arkana their freedom after the war.
